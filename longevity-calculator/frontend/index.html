About This Calculator

This calculator estimates life expectancy using CDC life tables and hazard ratios from peer-reviewed research (Li et al. 2018, Circulation).

Methodology

  • Base mortality from CDC 2019 life tables
  • Gompertz model for age-specific mortality
  • Hazard ratios from lifestyle epidemiology
  • Monte Carlo simulation for uncertainty

Lifestyle Factors

  • Smoking status
  • Physical activity level
  • Body Mass Index (BMI)
  • Sleep duration
  • Diet quality
  • Alcohol consumption

Disclaimer

This is a statistical estimate based on population averages. Individual outcomes vary greatly. This tool is for educational purposes and should not replace medical advice.
